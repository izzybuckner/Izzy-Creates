Shipping is CarMax's quoted fee to ship to the search area (22903), with the delivery window CarMax showed. Two cars were on hold for a shopper and show a pickup store instead of a shipping quote. All-in is price plus shipping, before tax, title and CarMax's processing fee.

Price drop means CarMax showed a struck-through earlier price. On hold means another shopper had it reserved. Popular was CarMax's own badge.

Vs CarMax norm is how far under (+) or over (-) the formula's price each car is, as a share of that price. Prices as listed on Nov 11, 2026. Listings change daily.

Price deal bands compare the asking price to the model's fair price: Great is 8% or more under, Good 3 to 8% under, Fair within 3%, High 3 to 8% over, Overpriced more than 8% over.

Dealer stars are the dealer's Google rating, which usually has hundreds or thousands of reviews. 4.5 is the typical rating among these dealers, so it is the zero point. CarGurus dealer reviews are fewer but come from car buyers, so they act as a check: after pulling a small review count toward a typical 3.9, a dealer under 3.4 on CarGurus loses 0.3 star and gets flagged. Google ratings were looked up for dealers within about 600 miles; the rest are scored as 4.3 and marked "Google n/a". Xtreme Autos Mall had no clear Google match.

All-in is the price plus a way to get the car to Charlottesville: CarMax's own transfer fee, nothing for a drive of 320 miles or less, otherwise a rough transport estimate of $400 + $0.60/mile (capped at $2,000). It leaves out tax, title and dealer doc fees.
